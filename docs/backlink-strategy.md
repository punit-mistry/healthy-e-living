# Ethical backlink and authority plan

Backlinks can't be generated by code. They're earned when a real person decides the site is worth pointing to. This plan focuses on three things Healthy E Living genuinely has: **a qualified clinician, useful free resources, and a local Mumbai presence.**

## 2. Outreach categories

The organisations below are real and relevant. **Before contacting any of them, check their current website, the right contact, and whether they accept contributions.** None have been contacted.

### Professional credibility (highest value for health sites)
- **Indian Dietetic Association (IDA)** — idaindia.com. If Irika is a member, ask whether members are listed in a directory or can contribute to their publications.
- **Her own training institution(s)** — alumni pages and features often link to graduates' practices. Fill in once qualifications are confirmed.
- **Hospitals or clinics she has worked with** — a "visiting consultant" or team listing is a natural, legitimate link.

### Healthcare referral partners (relationship-led, local)
- Gynaecologists (PCOS, pregnancy), endocrinologists and diabetologists, physiotherapists and sports clinics in Borivali, Malad, Kandivali, Dahisar, Mira Road and Bhayander.
- **Angle:** offer the printable checklist and balanced-plate guide for their waiting rooms, and a clear referral route. A link from their site's "partners" or "resources" page follows naturally.

### Legitimate directories (one honest profile each — no keyword stuffing)
- **Practo** — practo.com (doctor/dietitian profiles, widely used in India)
- **Google Business Profile** — see `google-search-console.md`, Step 8
- **Justdial** — justdial.com (local business listings)
- Keep the name, phone (WhatsApp +91 88790 43297) and website **identical** everywhere.

### Mumbai media (expert comment, not paid placement)
- **Mid-Day** (mid-day.com), **Free Press Journal** (freepressjournal.in), the Mumbai editions of **Hindustan Times** and **The Times of India**.
- **Angle:** seasonal, practical expert comment — monsoon food safety, eating well through Ganeshotsav and Diwali, commuter nutrition. Journalists look for quotable local experts; a short, sourced tip sheet sent ahead of the season works far better than a generic pitch.

### Health and lifestyle publications (expert contribution)
- **Healthshots** (healthshots.com), **Femina** (femina.in) and similar Indian wellness sections regularly quote dietitians.
- **Angle:** respond to specific stories with evidence-based input; offer an expert quote rather than asking for a link.
- Free journalist-request services (for example Qwoted, Help a B2B Writer) can surface requests for expert dietitian comment. Check each request's legitimacy before replying.

### Community and education
- Housing societies, schools (tiffin talks), running and cycling clubs, corporate wellness weeks. Talks often lead to a mention on the organiser's site or newsletter.
- Podcasts and Instagram Lives with other qualified health professionals. Research current Indian health podcasts before pitching; none are listed here because they haven't been verified.

## 3. How to pitch (template)

> **Subject:** Free printable for your [patients / members]: [resource name]
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm Irika Goyal, a clinical dietitian in Mumbai. I've put together [a one-page first-consultation checklist / an Indian balanced-plate guide] that [your patients/members] might find useful. It's free to print and share, and there's nothing to sign up for: [link].
>
> If it's useful, feel free to share it with [them]. And if you ever need a dietitian's perspective on [topic], I'm happy to help.
>
> Warm regards,
> Irika

Keep it short and personal, and make the value obvious. **Never ask for a link in exchange for anything.**

## 4. What not to do

- ❌ Buying links, or "guest post" packages sold on marketplaces
- ❌ Private blog networks or link exchanges with unrelated sites
- ❌ Automated outreach or comment spam
- ❌ Fake reviews or testimonials, or review gating
- ❌ Listings at addresses that aren't real places of business
- ❌ Dozens of thin directory profiles stuffed with keywords
- ❌ Invented "as featured in" logos or press mentions

Any of these can get the site penalised, and health sites are held to a higher standard ("Your Money or Your Life" content).

## 5. Rhythm

- **Monthly:** 5–10 genuine, personalised outreach messages, logged in the tracker.
- **Quarterly:** one new reviewed resource, plus a seasonal media tip sheet (monsoon, festive season, New Year).
- **Measure:** Search Console → **Links** → "Top linking sites". Quality matters far more than quantity.
